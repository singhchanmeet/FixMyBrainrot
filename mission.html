A quiet place to think

Fix My Brainrot exists for the small moments when you want to use your attention deliberately instead of handing it over to an endless feed.

The idea is intentionally modest. A few minutes of solving, remembering, reading, or learning will not transform a person overnight. But choosing an activity that asks something of you is different from passively consuming whatever appears next. That choice is the point.

What this project is

It is a collection of simple browser activities: no account, no score history, no advertisements, and no social comparison. The games are designed to be opened, used, and left behind whenever you are done.

What it is not

It is not a medical treatment, a productivity system, or a promise to improve intelligence. It does not measure your worth or turn attention into a competition. It is simply a place to practice paying attention to one thing at a time.

A deliberate absence of features

Progress resets on refresh. There are no streaks, achievements, notifications, or music. These omissions are part of the design: the activity should be enough on its own.
